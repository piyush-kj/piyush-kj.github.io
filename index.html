About me

I'm a third-year B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student at Lovely Professional University, Phagwara. Most of what I know, I learned by building real projects under real deadlines — specifically, through hackathons, where I've spent more than a few 36-hour stretches turning an idea into something that actually runs.

My interests sit at the meeting point of web development and applied AI. One project had me thinking about farmers and fair prices; another had me teaching a computer to recognise faces. What connects them is the same habit: understand the problem first, then pick the simplest tools that solve it well.

I care about writing code that people can actually use — and about being honest when I don't know something yet, then going and learning it.
